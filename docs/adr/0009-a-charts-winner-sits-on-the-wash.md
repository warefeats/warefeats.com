# A chart's winner sits on the wash

Amended by ADR 0010: a lead inside the noise puts every row tied with it on the wash, instead of none.

Each product's bars take its brand color, so a reader can follow one product from chart to chart, and that color outranks the red ADR 0008 took the winner's bar to be: with every candidate carrying one, nothing on a chart said who won. The winner's row now sits on the red wash, edge to edge across the chart and halfway into the gaps beside it, and every bar keeps its brand color. The wash is the mark ADR 0008 describes, under the same rules: it reads the value at the end of the bar, and a lead inside the noise gets none, so the wash, the method line, the winner line and the scorecard still agree. A box plot marks its fastest row the same way.

## Considered options

- **A red bar for the winner, over its brand color.** Rejected: a product's bar would change color from chart to chart, and a color would stop meaning one product.
- **A wash the height of the bar.** Rejected: it reads as a track behind the bar, a scale the chart doesn't have.
- **A grey wash.** Rejected: grey is the title band's ground and a quiet button's hover; red is the site's verdict color.
