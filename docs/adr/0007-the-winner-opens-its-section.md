# The winner opens its section

A reader meets the takeaway before the evidence. A post's section opens with its authored verdict and then its charts, stacked one per row with each test's title and description between them, and the post itself opens with its verdict under the deck. The matchup's section does the same with the line it computes: the winner, or the tie, opens the section above its charts, so both pages read in one order. The rest of ADR 0003 stands: the winner is the best mean among the candidates picked, a lead inside twice the standard error is a tie where samples repeat, the line and the scorecard's mark read the same column, the conditions and the scorecard close the page with nothing after it but the trademark notices, and a winner per chart stays deferred until the contract carries per-test samples.

## Considered options

- **Charts, then the winner, as ADR 0003 laid them out.** Rejected: with the charts stacked, the line sits a screen or more below the heading it answers, and the post and the matchup would read in opposite orders.
- **The winner line above the charts and again below them.** Rejected: the same line twice is clutter, and the scorecard already restates every column.
